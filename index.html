About Sam

I’ve spent my life around dogs, raising and caring for more than 8 of them, from a small Maltese Mix to a 130 lb Cane Corso, and every life stage from puppy to senior, with the oldest being 17.

My approach is calm, consistent, and praise-based. I pay close attention to each dog's body language and stress signals, and I keep owners informed every step of the way.

  • CPR certified
